# Phase 2 Section Contracts

| Section | Purpose | Allowed claims | Forbidden claims | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RSI definition | Fix scope and autonomy level | Harness/skill-level persistent change | Full L5 or weight self-training achieved | Consistent with research canon |
| Architecture | Define components and authority | Interfaces and planned behavior | Components already implemented unless Phase 1B evidence exists | Every component has input/output and owner |
| Data flow | Define provenance across rounds | Planned immutable records | Dataset exists or is licensed | Every transition has evidence pointer |
| Training flow | Define optimization stages | Planned prompt/tool/script optimization | Training completed or scores obtained | Baseline, split, seed and stopping rules present |
| Experiment plan | Define tests of H1-H5 | Expected comparisons | Expected outcomes stated as results | Each hypothesis maps to metrics and experiment IDs |
| Resource plan | Bound compute and cost | Hardware inventory and planning estimates | Guaranteed runtime/cost | Estimates labelled and formulas auditable |
